# Meeting Bingo 🎯

The only way to make your next corporate meeting bearable. Generate a random bingo card full of meeting buzzwords, mark them off as people say them, and celebrate when you get a line.

## How to Play

1. Open `index.html` before your next meeting
2. A random 5x5 bingo card appears with buzzwords
3. Click a square when someone says that phrase
4. Get five in a row (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal) to win
5. Try not to laugh when confetti fills your screen
6. Click "New Card" to play again

## Buzzwords Included

Synergy, circle back, deep dive, low-hanging fruit, move the needle, take it offline, loop in, bandwidth, pivot, touch base, on my radar, game changer, pain point, double down, unpack, drill down, alignment, scalable, ecosystem, north star, actionable, end of day, stakeholder, deliverable... and many more.
